Lumore — IPTV Player 1.8.1
Designed & developed by Mejri02.

A modern IPTV player for Android and Android TV. Connect your own Xtream Codes account,
Stalker portal or M3U/M3U8 playlist and watch live TV, movies and series.

Highlights
- 13 themes, including the new Lumore Aurora, Lumore Cinema and Lumore Neon layouts.
- Sleep timer, video scaling (Fit/Zoom/Stretch) and subtitle loading with sync.
- TV Guide with NOW/NEXT programmes, recent searches and indexed global search.
- Stalker portal support and a LibVLC fallback for RTSP/RTMP/UDP streams.
- Offline downloads with pause/resume, Wi-Fi-only mode and per-source favorites/progress.
- JSON backup and restore. All data stays on your device.

Build: open the project in Android Studio, or run
  gradle :app:assembleDebug
Requires JDK 17+ and Android SDK. compileSdk/targetSdk 36, minSdk 24.

Lumore does not provide or host any channels or subscriptions.
